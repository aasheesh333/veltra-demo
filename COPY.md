## 8. About Page

### Page hero
**Eyebrow:** About Veltra
**H1:** Energy is a promise.
**Subcopy:** We make it, move it and stand behind it — for India and the world.

### Story

Veltra began in 2019 with a single rooftop and a stubborn idea: clean energy should be simple to buy and boring to run. We started with solar, then added the pieces our customers kept asking for — storage, hydrogen, charging.

Today we operate across six countries, with more than 7 GW in play. We're still small enough to answer the phone. Still impatient enough to build faster than the market expects.

### Vision
A world where clean energy is the default, not the alternative.

### Mission
To deliver reliable clean energy at scale — and prove it every day, in every megawatt.

### Values
- **Do it properly.** Safety and quality are not negotiable.
- **Move first.** Speed matters when the climate does.
- **Stay accountable.** We own what we build.
- **Grow together.** Communities and customers come with us.

### Milestones
- **2019** — Veltra founded in Gurugram; first 5 MW rooftop commissioned.
- **2021** — First utility-scale solar park reaches 400 MW.
- **2022** — Green mobility arm launches with 1,000 charging points.
- **2024** — Coastal Hydrogen Hub breaks ground in Tamil Nadu.
- **2026** — 7.4 GW portfolio; operations in six countries.

## 9. Solutions Page

### Page hero
**Eyebrow:** What we do
**H1:** Three ways to go clean.
**Subcopy:** Renewable power, green hydrogen and electric mobility — built to work together.

### Renewable Energy

**Intro:** From site to switch-on, we develop and operate the power plants that keep industry running clean.

- Utility-scale solar, wind and hybrid plants
- Battery storage for round-the-clock supply
- Power purchase agreements up to 25 years
- In-house O&M with remote monitoring

### Hydrogen

**Intro:** We turn renewable electrons into green molecules, with plants designed for real industrial demand.

- Electrolyser plant design and construction
- Renewable power integration and storage
- Compression, storage and offtake logistics
- Safety, certification and compliance support

### Green Mobility

**Intro:** Charging infrastructure for the fleets already on the road — and the ones coming next.

- Highway and urban fast-charging networks
- Depot charging for buses and trucks
- Fleet management and uptime software
- Site design, permits and grid connections

### Delivery Process

1. **Listen** — We map your demand, site and timeline.
2. **Design** — Engineers shape the right mix and model the numbers.
3. **Build** — We construct, connect and commission.
4. **Run** — We operate, monitor and keep improving.
