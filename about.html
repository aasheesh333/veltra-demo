Our story

A stubborn idea, scaled.

Veltra began in 2019 with a single rooftop and a stubborn idea: clean energy should be simple to buy and boring to run. We started with solar, then added the pieces our customers kept asking for — storage, hydrogen, charging.

Today we operate across six countries, with more than 7 GW in play. We're still small enough to answer the phone. Still impatient enough to build faster than the market expects.
